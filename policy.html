🔒 Privacy Policy

Local Device Storage

Your cart items, wishlist, order history, and saved hostel room details are stored strictly in your own browser's localStorage.

Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not run ad trackers, do not sell user information, and do not share your details with any external marketing services.

WhatsApp Order Dispatch

The name, phone number, and room details you enter during checkout are used solely to deliver your order via WhatsApp (+91 72097 93015).

Full User Control

You can view, edit, or wipe all locally saved app data anytime with one tap using Clear App Data on the Profile page.
